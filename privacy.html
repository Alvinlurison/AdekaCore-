Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AdekaCore (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use information when you use our website and workspace.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We also store messages, files, events, and announcements you create inside the workspace.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to provide the AdekaCore workspace, identify members, and improve the platform. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services. Access is restricted to authenticated users of AdekaCore.

4. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and related data by contacting the Founder.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the AdekaCore team through the workspace or the founder’s registered email.
